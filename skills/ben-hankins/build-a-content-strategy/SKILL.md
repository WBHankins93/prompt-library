**When to use this skill:** content activity is inconsistent or high-volume but lacks a repeatable
theory connecting audience attention to trust, action, and business value.

**What it produces:** positioning, audience tensions, pillars, platform roles, recurring formats,
production and distribution system, experiments, and outcome-linked metrics.

## Start with the audience tension

Define the specific audience, desired change, recurring problem, current belief, and credible point
of view. Choose territory the creator can earn through experience or evidence. Content pillars
should resolve audience tensions, not mirror internal departments.

## Give each platform a role

Assign discovery, depth, conversation, conversion, or retention based on audience behavior and
format strengths. Adapt the idea to native consumption rather than cross-posting identical assets.
Avoid platform sprawl until one production-distribution loop is sustainable.

## Design repeatable formats

Create a small portfolio of formats with a recognizable promise: diagnosis, teardown, lesson,
story, proof, or practical tool. Define the opening tension, evidence, payoff, and next action.
Build series that compound familiarity while leaving room for live signal.

## Connect production to distribution

Set a cadence the available team can sustain, reuse research and core ideas across depths, and
reserve time for replies, collaboration, and redistribution. Engagement is part of content
delivery, not cleanup after publishing.

## Learn from the right metrics

Separate reach, retention, qualified engagement, owned-audience growth, and commercial action.
Compare formats over enough repetitions to reduce luck. Record the hypothesis before testing and
change one meaningful variable at a time.

## What good looks like

The audience can describe what the account is for, formats are recognizable without becoming
formulaic, and each platform has a deliberate job. Production remains sustainable, learning
accumulates across posts, and attention connects to trust or action rather than vanity alone.

## Hard rules

- NEVER chase a platform trend that contradicts the positioning.
- NEVER confuse reach with qualified audience growth.
- NEVER expand platforms before one sustainable loop works.
- ALWAYS design engagement and distribution into the system.
- ALWAYS judge formats across repeated tests, not one viral outlier.
